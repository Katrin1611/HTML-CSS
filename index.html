Why I love Trail Running so much

When I'm trail running:

  • I'm out in nature
  • I run on very different surfaces, at different paces and different slopes
  • usually, I have wonderful views, sometimes I see animals
  • I have to focus on the trail, no time for stress or annoying thoughts

Three major reasons why everybody should try Trail Running

  1. Running in nature releases stress, only from being in natural surroundins
  2. Trail runners don't care about pace: it doesn't matter if you run 4 min/km or 12 min/k
  3. Walking is part of the run: you can walk, you can pause and enjoy the views, you can take pictures; do whatever you feel like

And one special reason if you're still not convinced yet:

Trail runners love to eat lot's - and whatever they like
